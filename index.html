Candle care

Trim the wick to 1/4" before each use. Keep the candle within sight and burn it on a heat-resistant surface. Do not burn for more than 4 hours at a time. Keep away from flammable materials, children and pets. Discontinue use when 1/2" of wax remains.

Shipping & returns
